Hi, I'm Richel. This is the start of my little corner of the internet where I
write about what I'm learning and building in AI and machine learning, in plain
English.

## Why I write

I have a simple belief: the best ideas are wasted if people can't understand
them. So I spend a lot of time making complex things simple, whether that's in a
classroom, a YouTube video, or a post like this one.

Writing here does three things for me:

- **It sharpens my thinking.** If I can't explain it clearly, I don't understand
  it well enough yet.
- **It helps you.** The resources I've learned the most from were made by people
  one step ahead of me. I want to pay that forward.
- **It keeps me honest.** Writing about the work is a great way to actually
  finish it.

## What I'll write about

Expect posts on the things I work on every day:

1. **Forecasting and time series** — including the spatio-temporal work I find
   genuinely fun.
2. **RAG and AI assistants** — how to make them accurate, grounded, and useful.
3. **The statistician's mindset** — uncertainty, experiment design, and why
   rigor still matters.

> Richel makes tech easy. That's the whole goal, and it's what every post here
> is trying to do.

If that sounds useful, [join the newsletter](/writing) so you don't miss new
posts. Thanks for reading. More soon.
